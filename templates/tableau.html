Visualize the information with Tableau
Tableau Desktop is used for visualizing and analyzing data. It allows you to create interactive charts, dashboards, and data visualizations that will help you interact with analyzed data effectively.

Steps

  1. Download Tableau Desktop and log in to your personal account.
  2. In the Connect tab, click on PostgreSQL in the To a Server section.
  3. In the window that opens, fill in the fields with the corresponding values:

    Server: localhost

    Port: 5432

    Database: datamining_db

    Username: postgres

    Password: 1234

  4. Done! You will be able to visualize the data from the repository you just analyzed in whatever way suits you best thanks to the variety of charts offered by Tableau.
